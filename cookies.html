Cookies and local storage

Last updated: 28 October 2026

harbour.ryst.in and the RYST Harbour web app set no analytics or advertising cookies and load no trackers. This page lists everything the site keeps in your browser, and the third parties that may set a cookie when you use a feature that needs them. Because all of it is strictly necessary to run the service, there is no cookie banner.

1. What the site keeps in your browser

All of these are in your browser’s local storage (not cookies), stay on this device only, and are never sent to anyone but our own server.

ItemWhat it doesHow long
ryst_proxy_token, ryst_user_email, ryst_user_role, ryst_user_admin, ryst_user_modulesKeeps you signed in to your villa’s app and remembers what your role may open.Until you sign out; the token itself expires after 24 hours.
harbour_console_tokenKeeps a Harbour team member signed in to the Harbour console.Until sign-out.
ryst_dash_view, ryst_lang, other ryst_* preferencesRemembers choices such as the caretaker view or Tamil/English.Until cleared.
ryst_demoMarks that you are using the demo villa.Until you leave the demo.
Push subscriptionIf you turn on notifications, your browser gives our server an address to send them to.Until you turn notifications off.

2. Third parties that may set cookies

  • Google sign-in (accounts.google.com) on the sign-in pages: Google sets its own cookies to sign you in, under Google’s privacy policy. Nothing from Google loads on other pages; fonts are served from our own site.
  • Razorpay when you pay for a subscription: its checkout sets cookies to run and secure the payment (Razorpay’s privacy policy).

3. Villa booking websites

Each villa’s own booking website (for example stay.ryst.in) is the villa’s, and may use analytics or advertising cookies with its visitors’ consent. Those sites have their own cookie policies.

4. Your choices

You can clear this site’s data in your browser at any time; you will simply need to sign in again. Blocking local storage stops the app from keeping you signed in.

5. Questions

Email info@dreamour.in. Our privacy policy covers everything else we do with personal data.
