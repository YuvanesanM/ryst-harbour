1. The service

RYST Harbour is software for running independent villas: bookings and calendar sync with online travel agencies, guest messages, invoices, a direct booking website, staff tools, reports and related features. It is offered to businesses, not to consumers for personal use. What each plan includes is shown on our pricing page.

2. Accounts and your team

  • You sign in with Google. Keep your Google account secure; anything done from your account is your responsibility.
  • You decide who on your team gets access and what they can see, and you remove people who should no longer have it.
  • You must be 18 or older, and the information you give us must be accurate.

3. Free trial

Each new villa starts with a free trial for the period shown on our pricing page and at sign-up (currently 14 days). Nothing is charged during the trial and no card is needed. If you don’t choose a plan by the end of it, your account becomes read-only until you do. You may start a trial yourself from our website, or with an invite link from us; either way one trial per villa and per Google account.

4. Plans, fees and payment

  • Fees: a monthly or annual subscription per villa, plus a one-time setup fee, as shown on the pricing page. GST is charged on top at the applicable rate (currently 18%).
  • Billing: subscriptions are paid in advance through Razorpay and renew automatically each month or year until cancelled. Annual plans are priced at 10 months.
  • Minimum term: subscriptions have a 6-month minimum term.
  • Late payment: if a renewal fails, you have 7 days to pay. After that the account becomes read-only until payment is made. Your data is not deleted.
  • Other costs: your own domain name, your payment gateway’s fees (for guest payments) and WhatsApp message charges are paid by you directly to those providers.
  • Price changes: we will give at least 30 days’ notice before a new price applies to your next renewal.

Refunds are covered by our refund and cancellation policy.

5. Cancelling

You can cancel with 30 days’ notice, by email to info@dreamour.in or through your account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period in which the notice ends, and not before the minimum term ends. You keep access until then.

  • Leaving: after your account ends you can export your data for 30 days. We then delete it within 90 days, except records we must keep by law.

For personal data you put into RYST Harbour about your guests and staff, you are the data fiduciary and we process it on your behalf under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will:

The full terms of this processing — including the list of sub-processors, breach notification, international transfers and the clauses that apply to villas in the EU or UK — are in our data processing addendum, which forms part of these terms.

Guest ID documents. Online check-in can collect a photo of the lead guest’s government ID for the register the law requires of you. RYST Harbour deletes that photo automatically one year after check-out (you can shorten this to 30 days or lengthen it in Settings); the register entry stays with the booking. You decide which IDs you accept and you must not require more than the law allows — for example, never demand a full Aadhaar number when a masked Aadhaar or other ID will do, and never use Aadhaar authentication through RYST Harbour.

Messaging consent. WhatsApp and Instagram messages are sent through your own Meta-approved numbers and templates. You are responsible for having the recipient’s consent for promotional messages, as Meta’s policies and the law require. RYST Harbour records when a guest replies STOP and will not send promotional templates to that number until they reply START; messages about a booking they hold still go. You must not use RYST Harbour to get around that.

Automated and AI-drafted messages. RYST Harbour can answer guests automatically and can draft replies with an AI model for you to review. You decide what is switched on, you are responsible for what is sent from your account. AI drafts can be wrong: read them before you approve them.
