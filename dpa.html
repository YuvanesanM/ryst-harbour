Data processing addendum

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Part of the terms of service

This addendum sets out how Dreamour Multiventures (“Harbour”, “we”) processes personal data on behalf of each villa that uses RYST Harbour (“you”). You are the data fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (and the controller under the GDPR or UK GDPR where those apply to you); we are your data processor. It applies automatically to every villa account; you do not need to sign it.

1. What we process, and why

  • Whose data: your guests and enquirers, your staff and team, and people who message your villa.
  • What data: names, phone numbers, email addresses, stay dates and guest counts, quotes, invoices and payment references, check-in records including a photo of the lead guest’s government ID and a signature, food orders, feedback, and messages on WhatsApp and Instagram.
  • Why: only to provide RYST Harbour to you — bookings and calendar sync, messaging, invoices and payments, check-in, reports and alerts — and as the law requires of us.
  • How long: while your account is active, then as set out in section 7.

2. Your instructions

We process personal data only on your documented instructions: these terms, the settings you choose in the app, and the actions you and your team take in it. If we believe an instruction breaks the law we will tell you. You are responsible for the lawfulness of the data you collect, for giving guests the notices the law requires, and for any consent (for example for promotional messages).

3. Our people

Only Harbour staff who need access to support you can see your data, and they are bound by confidentiality. We do not use your guests’ data for our own purposes, and never for advertising.

4. Security

We use HTTPS everywhere; signed, expiring sign-in tokens; separation of each villa’s data; private file storage that needs a signed link; encrypted payment and messaging keys; access by role; nightly backups; and server logs kept for no more than 90 days. We review these measures as the service changes.

5. Sub-processors

We use the following providers to run RYST Harbour. We give villa owners at least 30 days’ notice in the app or by email before adding a new sub-processor that will handle guest data; if you object on reasonable grounds and we cannot resolve it, you may end your subscription without penalty.

6. International transfers

Some providers store or process data outside India. We transfer data only as Indian law permits. If you are established in the EU or UK, the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses (processor-to-processor and controller-to-processor modules, as applicable) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum are incorporated into this addendum for transfers to us and our sub-processors in countries without an adequacy decision; email us for a signed copy.

7. Returning and deleting data

  • While your account is active you can export your data from the app at any time.
  • After your account ends you can export it for 30 days; we then delete it within 90 days, except records we must keep by law (invoices and payment records, up to 8 years).
  • Guest ID photos are deleted one year after check-out by default (30 days to 10 years, as you set in Settings). Deleted data leaves backups as they are replaced.

8. Helping you with guests’ rights and the law

We help you answer guests who ask to see, correct or delete their data, or who withdraw consent, through the app’s tools and, where needed, by email within 10 working days. We help you with data protection assessments and with inquiries from the Data Protection Board of India or an EU/UK supervisory authority, as far as they concern our processing.

9. Breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data we will tell you without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours, with what we know: what happened, whose data, the likely consequences and what we are doing. You remain responsible for notifying the Data Protection Board of India, affected guests and, where applicable, EU/UK authorities; we will give you what you need to do so.

10. Showing we comply

On request, no more than once a year unless a breach or an authority requires it, we will give you the information needed to show that we meet this addendum, including our sub-processors’ security documentation, and answer reasonable questions. Where that is not enough, you may have an audit carried out by an independent auditor we both accept, at your cost, on 30 days’ notice and during business hours.

11. Liability and term

Liability under this addendum is as set out in the terms of service. This addendum lasts as long as we process personal data for you. If there is a conflict between this addendum and the terms, this addendum prevails for the processing of personal data.
