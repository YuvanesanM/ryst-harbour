    Availability auto-reply

    When a guest asks about dates on WhatsApp (for example "is 25–27 Dec free for 10?"), Interakt answers straight away from the live calendar: free or booked with the nearest free dates, the price for their group and a booking link with the dates filled in. Other messages are left for you, and you get a Telegram alert for each reply. Needs the Interakt webhook below with Message received ticked in Interakt. Saves as soon as you tick it.

    It also answers month and festival questions ("any weekends in November?", "Deepavali?" — from your holiday names under Tariffs), and when a guest replies BOOK it sends a Razorpay payment link for the advance right in the chat (valid 30 minutes, dates held meanwhile; FULL for the whole amount with your full-payment discount). Payments confirm the booking automatically. A quote (channel WhatsApp) is made when they reply BOOK; if they haven't paid 2 hours later you get a Telegram nudge to follow up. Every enquiry stays in Review below. When you reply to a guest yourself in Interakt, auto-replies to them pause for 6 hours; each Telegram alert also has a pause link. In a chat, type #auto to turn them back on for that guest, or #pause to stop them for 7 days. Messages about cancelling, refunds, changes or payments, and date questions from guests who already have a booking, are always left for you — with a Telegram alert.

    Instagram DMs

    Automated sending

    When configured, the templates below go out automatically on a schedule (check-in requests, stay reminders, feedback requests, and booking confirmations) instead of needing a manual click from Bookings. A scenario with an Interakt template name set below (once that template is approved by Meta) sends for real, straight to the guest's WhatsApp — no tap needed. Every other scenario still falls back to a Telegram message with a one-tap link that opens WhatsApp pre-filled, so the actual send is you, from your own number.

    Delivery status webhook
    
    
    Interakt only says a message was accepted; whether it was actually delivered, read or failed comes later through this webhook. Paste the URL into Interakt → Settings → Developer Settings → Webhook, and tick the message API events (sent, delivered, read, failed). Bookings then shows Delivered ✓✓ / Read ✓✓ / Not delivered next to each send, and a failed send alerts Telegram with WhatsApp's reason. Keep this URL private — the key in it is what lets Interakt post here.
    
    
    
      
    
    
    
    

    
    Telegram notify setup
    
    
    One-time setup on Render: message @BotFather on Telegram, send /newbot, and set the token it gives you as TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN (a Render environment variable — not set from here). Each person who wants alerts then taps 🔔 Get alerts on Telegram on the home screen and presses Start in Telegram — that's all; their chat is saved against their own name. Which alerts each person gets is set in the Team tab.
